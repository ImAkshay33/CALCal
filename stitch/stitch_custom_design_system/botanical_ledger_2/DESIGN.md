# CALCal Design System: Botanical Ledger (Refined)

## Philosophy & Tone
Calm, confident, food-first health tracking tailored for Indian dietary profiles. Clean structure, grounded natural materials (linen beige, forest green, warm ink), with restrained focal accents.

## Color Palette
- page-background: `#e7dfcf` (Linen beige)
- card-background: `#f0eadc` (Lighter warm beige)
- header-panel: `#1f3a2b` (Deep forest green)
- header-text: `#f5f2eb` (Soft ivory)
- ink-primary: `#1a2620` (Deep charcoal ink)
- ink-secondary: `#55635a` (Muted herbal slate)
- border-subtle: `#ded6c4` (Warm border outline)
- action-primary: `#7b2cff` (Electric purple - reserved strictly for primary CTA action buttons)
- action-text: `#ffffff`
- status-safe / under-limit: `#2d7a48` (Natural forest green for within-budget / positive)
- status-danger / exceeded: `#c83939` (Clear red for exceeding limits, warnings, inline errors)

## Typography
- Headings & Numeral Focal Points: Bricolage Grotesque (Bold, semi-expanded)
- Body, Labels & UI: Instrument Sans / Inter (Clean, geometric, sentence-case)
- Eyebrow labels: Strictly sentence-case (no all-caps)
- Large numerals are the hero focal elements

## Geometry & Elevation
- Border radius: 14px to 24px (curved organic cards)
- Shadows: None on static cards; soft subtle shadow on floating buttons only
- Zero gradients, no stock food photos, minimal clean SVG micro-icons
